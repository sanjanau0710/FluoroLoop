FluoroLoop

PFAS Treatment & Fluorine Fate Simulator

Follow the fluorine. Not just the PFAS.

FluoroLoop is an interactive, research-informed educational tool designed to explore PFAS destruction methods, compare treatment pathways, and examine what happens to fluorine after treatment.

Inspiration

PFAS, often called forever chemicals, are difficult to destroy because of their strong carbon-fluorine bonds. While researching emerging PFAS destruction technologies, I became interested in a question: What happens to the fluorine after PFAS are broken down?

That question inspired FluoroLoop, a tool that explores both PFAS treatment and the potential for fluorine recovery.

What It Does

Compares six PFAS treatment pathways.

Evaluates treatment options using seven comparison metrics.

Visualizes possible fluorine fate outcomes.

Allows users to explore different treatment scenarios and priorities.

Explains the tradeoffs behind treatment rankings.

How I Built It

I developed FluoroLoop using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, combining interactive web development with concepts from environmental chemistry and PFAS research.

Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was translating complex chemical processes into an understandable interactive experience without presenting illustrative calculations as experimentally validated results.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of PFAS destruction, fluorine fate, environmental engineering, and interactive software development.

What's Next

I hope to expand FluoroLoop by incorporating validated experimental datasets, additional treatment pathways, and more detailed fluorine recovery models.

Important Note

FluoroLoop is a research-informed educational prototype. Its numerical scores and fluorine percentages are illustrative, not experimentally validated performance predictions.
